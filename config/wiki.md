
### 📖 EN 378 Reference Guide

#### Access Categories

* **Category a (General Access):** Rooms or buildings accessible to the general public (e.g., Hospitals, courts, supermarkets, schools, hotels).
* **Category b (Supervised Access):** Rooms or buildings with restricted access, usually occupied by instructed personnel (e.g., Offices, business premises, laboratories).
* **Category c (Authorized Access):** Access restricted to authorized persons only, who are acquainted with safety precautions (e.g., Manufacturing facilities, machinery rooms, cold stores).

---

#### Location Classes

* **Class I:** Equipment within the occupied space.
* **Class II:** Compressors are located in a separate machinery room or outdoors, but some refrigerant-containing parts may be in the occupied space.
* **Class III:** All refrigerant-containing parts are situated in a machinery room or open air. The machinery room shall fulfil the requirements of EN 378-3.
* **Class IV:** Ventilated enclosures. This enclosure shall fulfil the requirements of EN 378-2 and EN 378-3 (see below).

---

#### Refrigerant Characteristics

* **Practical limit:** Concentration used for simplifiedcalculation to determine the maximum refrigerant charge in an occupied space.
* **ATEL/ODL limit:** Acute-toxicity exposure limit / oxygen deprivation limit, exposure limits defined in EN 378.

---

#### Other Definitions

* **Machinery room:** Enclosed room or space, with mechanical ventilation, sealed from public areas and not accessible to the public, which is intended to contain components of the refrigerating system.
* **Sealed system:** Refrigerating system in which all refrigerant containing parts are made tight by welding, brazing or a similar permanent connection.
* **Auto-ignition temperature:** Lowest temperature at or above which a chemical can spontaneously combust in a normal atmosphere without external ignition (flame or spark).

---

#### Operational requirements for ventilation

* Ventilation shall run all the times, the airflow shall be monitered and the refrigerating system shall switch into safe mode within 10 s if the airflow is below Q_min or,
* Ventilation shall be switched on by a refrigerant gas sensor before 25% of the LFL is reached. Sensor and ventilation shall be checked regularly.
